GroupDrive India

Buy your car together. Dealers compete for a group of ready buyers.

The problem

Individual car buyers have little negotiating power and face opaque, hard-to-compare dealer discounts. Dealers spend heavily to acquire each lead and cannot tell serious buyers from browsers.

How it works

Buyers join a campaign for a specific car variant in their city with a small refundable deposit. When the target (for example 20 buyers) is reached, dealers in the city bid with a discount plus freebies. The best live offer wins; if no offer comes, deposits are refunded.

Who it is for

  • First-time and upgrade car buyers in metros and tier-2 cities
  • Dealerships wanting pre-qualified, ready-to-buy demand
  • Corporate groups and housing societies buying together
