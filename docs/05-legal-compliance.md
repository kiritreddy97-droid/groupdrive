# GroupDrive India: Legal and Compliance Checklist

This is a working checklist, not legal advice. Have an Indian lawyer review it before launch.

- Hold deposits through a payment-aggregator escrow/nodal arrangement; do not co-mingle
- Dealer terms: offers are binding for the validity period
- Consumer Protection Act e-commerce rules for disclosures and grievance officer
- Buyer data shared with dealers only after the buyer opts in (DPDP Act, 2023)
- GST on platform fees

## Always
- Privacy policy and terms of service before collecting any real user data.
- A grievance officer and a data-deletion path for users.
- Entity setup (e.g. private limited company), GST registration when thresholds apply.
