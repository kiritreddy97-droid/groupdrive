# GroupDrive India: Business Plan

> Buy your car together. Dealers compete for a group of ready buyers.

## Origin of the idea
Idea from a creator video: instead of one buyer negotiating alone, a platform gathers buyers for the same car in a city, tells dealers '20 ready buyers', compares offers and shows potential savings.

## Problem
Individual car buyers have little negotiating power and face opaque, hard-to-compare dealer discounts. Dealers spend heavily to acquire each lead and cannot tell serious buyers from browsers.

## Solution
Buyers join a campaign for a specific car variant in their city with a small refundable deposit. When the target (for example 20 buyers) is reached, dealers in the city bid with a discount plus freebies. The best live offer wins; if no offer comes, deposits are refunded.

## Target users
- First-time and upgrade car buyers in metros and tier-2 cities
- Dealerships wanting pre-qualified, ready-to-buy demand
- Corporate groups and housing societies buying together

## Revenue model
- Success fee per car delivered, paid by the dealer
- Featured-dealer subscription
- Finance and insurance referrals
- Accessories marketplace (phase 3)

## Key risks
- Dealers and OEM franchise agreements may restrict group discounts
- Buyers walk away after winning, so the dealer loses trust
- Deposit handling requires a compliant payments flow
- Cold start: need buyers before dealers care, dealers before buyers trust
- Fake buyers inflating counts

## Success metrics
- Buyers per campaign and time-to-threshold
- Dealer bid participation rate
- Average savings vs list
- Buyer conversion to delivery
- Success fee per car
